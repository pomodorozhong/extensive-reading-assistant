    Which level should I choose?

    Choose a level where reading feels easy and you know almost every word. Extensive reading works best when you can enjoy the story without stopping often.

    A1Very short, simple sentences about familiar things like family, food, and home.
    A2Everyday phrases and simple stories about daily life.
    B1Clear stories on familiar topics, with most everyday words.
    B2Longer stories with some abstract ideas and less common words.
    C1Detailed, complex writing with idioms and a wide vocabulary.
    C2Almost anything, including subtle and literary language.
    If you keep stopping to look up words, try a lower level.
